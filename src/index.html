A Letter To My Daisy

April 11th, 2021 - 3:23PM

This will probably be a long ramble since i'm freestyling this letter. i think they're more enjoyable this way.

I didn't plan for this to be a birthday gift. I got an idea to make a logo or a website when your parents came to vist and we had a conversation about Bake-off. It made sense in my head that if they picked you to be on it, you would need a website and a logo.

I started working on this on the 5th of April, I drew a bullshit little sketch of what i thought the finish logo and website would look like but I realized i needed to start over again a couple days before your birthday because i wasn't feeling what i had on the page so far.

A conversation we had popped into my head. We were on our walk to the river beach and you pointed out the magnolia trees and the color that someone painted their front door.

I remember asking you what color you would paint your front door? and you said "Probably yellow, royal purple or bright orange"

I realized there was none of that on the page. Not the colors, the creativity or the personality of Daisy anywhere (there probably still isn't as much Daisy personality now but it's a start). I was building some bullshit generic corporate style website and i realized that wasn't you.

I wanted some of the elements on the website to remind you of the old house and some stuff from the new one. The butter dish, not as floraly as your sisters but a butter dish still. The social media icons are supposed to be those flour pots that you had to give up when we moved over and the wooden shelf holding them up is the one from the old house.

The reason i bring this conversation up is because sometimes i had tendency to do what i think is best for you without thinking of what you want and how important it is for me to let you find yourself and be your own creative person.

This year has been a tough year for both us with the move and dealing with the bathroom for the last couple of months. I realize that i put a lot of pressure on myself to try to keep you safe and happy but i might just need to listen and talk about it some more

I've loved spending the last year stuck with you, for the most part it's been very stress free. The future is still pretty uncertain as to where we are going to end up in but we've handled this year pretty well soooo i'll follow you wherever we end up.

Everything on here is a work in progress and there's no pressure to continue to keep building on this. It was just an unexpected project that came out of a wonderful walk and a conversation on the way down to the river beach.

I LOVE YOU LOTS
- Leo
